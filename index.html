The workshop

One pair of hands, a lot of disciplines.

The fun of GMS is the range. Some weeks it's wool, some weeks it's leather, some weeks it's a 3D printer running overnight. Here's what tends to come out of the studio.

Knitting
Garments and accessories, from cast-on to blocked.
Sewing
Pouches, bags and inserts, machine and by hand.
Leatherwork
Saddle-stitched journals, cases and small goods.
Bookbinding
Saddle-stitch and beyond, with printed inserts.
3D printing
Jigs, fixtures and parametric designs in OpenSCAD.
Natural dyeing
Colour from the woodland, the garden and the foraging basket.
Weaving
Small-loom pieces and woven trims.
Foraging
Gathering what the seasons offer, then putting it to use.
Cement work
Cast vessels and homeware for the studio and the house.

Hello

I'm Gill.

I've spent a long working life in technology, and an equally long one making things with my hands. Gill Makes Stuff is where the two meet — a place to share projects properly, materials, mistakes and all.

Home is a house my partner and I built in a birch woodland in the Scottish Highlands, with a studio built for exactly this kind of work. If something can be made rather than bought, I'd usually rather make it.
